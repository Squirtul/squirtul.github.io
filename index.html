EBKT - Kortrijk



Both IFR and VFR flights are permitted at EBKT.


View All Charts.



Air Traffic Services


Kortrijk is within class G airspace. It does not receive air traffic control (ATC) services.


Kortrijk is instead served by an Aerodrome Flight Information Service (AFIS). They will provide information, but separation is entirely pilots' responsibility.


Kortrijk Information (EBKT_I_TWR) is on the frequency 120.255.



Airspace


EBKT is within a RMZ/TMZ (radio + transponder mandatory zone).


All aircraft therefore must be in contact with EBKT_I_TWR when within the RMZ/TMZ, and must have a mode S transponder turned on.


EBMO (Moorsele)'s paradrop area partially covers the north side of the RMZ/TMZ. EBKT_I_TWR will inform if this area is active.


Restricted airspace EBR25 covers parts of the approach onto runway 06. Pilots may fly through it to land at EBKT.



Parking


Apron 2 is the primary parking area. Stands 210 and 250 can facilitate aircraft with a wingspan of less than 36m. All other stands ending in a 1 or 3 require a wingspan less than 17m. All other stands ending in a 2 or 4 require a wingspan less than 24m.


Apron 1 is used for general aircraft. It has a max wingspan of 15m and cannot take jet nor turboprop aircraft.


Apron 3 is used for helicopters. It has a max wingspan of 15m and max D-value of 13m.


There are various hangars across the field. These are used by independent companies and flight schools and are to be used at pilot discretion.


View the Parking Chart.



IFR Departure


All IFR must be PBN (performance based navigation), as no conventional IFR is available.


IFR clearances are issued by EBBU_W_CTR but relayed by EBKT_I_TWR.


Cat A and B aircraft can expect an A departure from runway 24 and a C departure from runway 06.


Cat C aircraft can expect a B departure from runway 24 and a C departure from runway 06.


IFR departures climb to 3000ft, where they must contact EBBU_W_CTR for clearance to enter controlled airspace.


An IFR pickup when entering controlled airspace is possible.


Only 1 departure may be on a SID at one time.


Departures are manually handed off by EBKT_I_TWR.



VFR Entry/Exit


Aircraft must enter and leave the RMZ/TMZ via the mandatory reporting points N1, E1, S1 and W1. They are also advised to use the recommended reporting points N2, E2, S2 and W2.


Routes via N and W conflict with IFR procedures. It is the pilots' responsibility to maintain separation.


Upon leaving the RMZ/TMZ, flight information is available with Brussels Information (EBBU_I_CTR) on 126.900.



Local VFR


3 circuits are used at EBKT.


The general circuit is used by aircraft less than 2T. It is a left-hand pattern at 1000ft AMSL.


The helicopter circuit is used by helicopters. It is a right-hand pattern at 1000ft AMSL.


The high performance circuit is used by aircraft greater than 2T. It is always to the south of the field for both runways 06 and 24, at 1500ft AMSL.


To join the circuit, aircraft should proceed from either the mandatory or recommended reporting point (if applicable) to the downwind of their relevant circuit.


Aim not to fly overhead the signalling area.


Multiple circuits can and will be used simultaneously. While traffic information will be provided by EBKT_I_TWR, separation is ultimately pilots' discretion.



Pushback


All stands are taxi-out, hence no pushbacks are required.



Taxi


Ensure you are squawking mode C before requesting taxi information.


Aircraft are required to contact EBKT_I_TWR prior to taxi, where they'll be given relevant information on current traffic and conditions.


Taxi routing is done at pilot discretion.


It is mandatory to contact EBKT_I_TWR before lineup, before crossing the runway and when vacating the runway after both crossing and landing.


Aircraft with a wingspan of 24m or greater can only taxi via B, between B2 and B3. They must backtrack thereafter.


All aircraft must depart full length.



IFR Arrival


Aircraft must fly via ARWEF to begin all instrument approaches.


Pilots must report their ETO (estimated time over) ARWEF to EBKT_I_TWR at least 10 mins prior.


If with another controller, pilots must request their ETO ARWEF to be sent to EBKT_I_TWR, or request a frequency change. They may return to their original frequency after reporting to EBKT.


When leaving controlled airspace, aircraft must contact EBKT_I_TWR for information. Pilots may request radar-based information from EBBU_I_CTR on 126.900 when far from EBKT.


Only 1 aircraft may be on an RNP approach and only one may be holding at ARWEF simultaneously.


For runway 06, reporting ARWEF, GIGAD, IKIFE and MINLU is mandatory.


For runway 24, reporting ARWEF, MIRZO and ALFAS is mandatory.


The RNP for runway 06 is offset by 15° from the extended runway centreline.


Pilots must report a go-around to EBKT_I_TWR and report overhead ARWEF once again.


Pilots must report once they have vacated the runway.
